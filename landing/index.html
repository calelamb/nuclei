Features

Everything you need to learn quantum

Code Editor

Monaco-powered editor with quantum-aware syntax highlighting, autocomplete, and inline diagnostics.

Circuit Visualization

Live-rendered circuit diagrams that update as you type. Every gate and wire, visualized instantly.

Bloch Sphere

Interactive 3D visualization of qubit states. Rotate, zoom, and explore state space in real time.

Probability Display

Measurement probabilities update live after each simulation. Understand outcomes at a glance.

Dirac AI Assistant

An AI tutor that sees your code, circuit, and errors. Ask anything about quantum computing.

Multi-Framework

Write in any major quantum SDK — Qiskit, Cirq, CUDA-Q, or Q#. Nuclei auto-detects from imports.
